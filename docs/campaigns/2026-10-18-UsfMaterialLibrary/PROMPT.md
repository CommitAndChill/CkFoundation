Written 2026-10-18; revised 2026-10-19 by the user's explicit breadth-first clarification, which supersedes the industrial-first emphasis of the host continuation prompt. Moving state is in PROGRESS.md; roster and batch contracts are in PLAN.md.

Build a broad, visually convincing, texture-backed library of reusable game materials. Prioritize distinct families and clean, intact surfaces. Wear, dirt, rust and damage are optional variations. Existing industrial painted steel is a supporting example, not the collection's identity or a prerequisite for expansion. First batch: silk, pebbled leather, glazed ceramic tile/contrasting grout, finished oak, brushed aluminum and matte molded plastic.

Use appropriately licensed base-color, tangent-normal and roughness textures where suitable, plus metallic, AO, height, opacity or other useful maps. Procedural shading complements the texture library; generic colors/noise do not substitute for it. Every material has a defining visual identity and suitable inputs/settings. Texture sampling, normal strength, roughness, color, UV scale and relevant specialized properties remain live-editable through .ush and existing parameter bindings. Adding a new texture binding may require asset regeneration; editing sampling/math for existing bindings must not.

Preserve desktop deferred Substrate Blendable, native Toon, named Specimen/CoreBase assignment, repaired specimen axes/UVs, open rows and accepted studio brightness. Region blending is distinct from physical slab layering. No Adaptive migration or engine work is implicit. No publication is authorized.

Acceptance prioritizes visual quality and diversity: texture provenance and editable sources, recognizable family-specific responses, sensible physical scale, useful clean defaults, close and gameplay captures, same-instance entrypoint/shared-helper edit and exact recovery, actual cost evidence, compatible focused tests and user artistic inspection. Recolored presets do not count as new material families. Establish excellent individual surfaces before selected combinations and optional aging; layering supports the library.
