# USF material studio

Written 2026-10-18. Stable brief; status is in PROGRESS.md. Retire this brief after
the studio workflow is accepted and documented beside its authoring resources.

Improve the USF Materials gym with attractive source-authored materials and a
standard logo-free material specimen. Preserve live .ush iteration for all normally
presented materials. Avoid statue-based displays. Ask Astra for design and review.

Use the existing CkUsf LookDefinition/generator/reload path, CkUsf gym control panel,
and local lighting precedent from CkPixelArtGym_JudgeScene. Introduce no shader
architecture or engine changes. Shared lighting reload stays explicit.

The first slice comprises ivory ceramic, satin copper, burgundy lacquer, graphite
rubber, pale green wax, and existing native Toon. Use a neutral core/backdrop and
consistent direct lights. Display image-like looks on planes and retain the sphere
for WPO. Keep edge outline opt-in. Asset-specific licensing and provenance must be
recorded; do not infer mesh rights from a renderer's software license.

Success means a verified clean specimen silhouette without an Unreal logo, useful
material distinction under the same exposure/light rig, and a .ush save updating
the existing material in PIE. Human visual acceptance is required.
